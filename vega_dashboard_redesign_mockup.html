What changed & why (the science)

Numbers right-aligned, tabular figures. Magnitude reads from digit position — columns become vertical bar charts you scan in one pass. (Tufte / Few)
Color means one thing. Green & red appear only on money (P/L, wins). Everything else is neutral ink so the eye isn’t pulled by decoration.
Gate dots, not words. 8 dots in fixed order — filled = pass, ringed = fail. Which gates failed is now a shape you glance at, not a list you read. Fill/shape works without color (8% of men are red-green colorblind).
One hero number per row. Priority is largest + backed by a length bar (preattentive magnitude) + a ▼ marks it as the sort key. Everything else steps down the type scale.
Confidence is visible. The scorecard greys stats that only have n=1 behind them and shows a 1-of-30 progress bar — the interface signals how much to trust each number.
Hero card + micro-interactions. The day’s best setup is lifted out (Von Restorff); Log pulses + toasts; Rescan spins. Feedback under 400ms keeps you in flow.
