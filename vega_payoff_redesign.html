Payoff column — redesign

Rebuilt to match the Robinhood P/L chart you sent: shaded green profit / orange loss zones split at the zero line, a “price now” line cutting through, break-even / max-profit / max-loss dots, and — the key upgrade — a current-standing readout so each row tells you where you stand, not just the abstract shape. Orange (not red) for loss follows Robinhood and is easier for red-green colorblind users.

On click — the full risk graph (two modes)

Why two modes. For a candidate you haven’t entered, there’s no P/L yet — so the headline shows cushion (how far price can fall before your break-even) and the price-now dot sits on the max-profit plateau. For an open position, the same chart shows live unrealized P/L with the dot on the curve at the current mark — exactly Robinhood’s use. VEGA already tracks current_mark and unrealized_net, so the position mode needs no new data.
