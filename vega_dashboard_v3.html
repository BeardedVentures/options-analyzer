How to read the grade

STRONG — a fat pitch: all 8 gates clean, ≥4 independent signals aligned (real rich IV, comfortable distance, deep liquidity, trend), no red flags. Rare on purpose.
SOLID — a clean, gate-passing trade with good probability, but one axis short of a fat pitch (often: IV is still an estimate).
MARGINAL — takeable only with a caveat: thin premium, fails a gate, POP near the floor, or estimated data. The “why” names the catch.
SKIP — fails multiple gates or carries stacked red flags.
